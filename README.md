### 👋 About me

I build e-commerce checkouts. At **[Trinio](https://trinio.co)** I work across the whole path a purchase takes: the checkout the shopper sees, the plugin that runs it inside VTEX stores, and the Node.js services behind it. Before that I spent about two years on headless commerce with [deco.cx](https://deco.cx), in its open-source apps and on real storefronts.

I started out as an electronics technician, which is where I picked up the habit of chasing a bug down to its root cause instead of patching the symptom.

### 🛒 What I build at Trinio

- **Cashback and loyalty.** I own the integrations with the cashback and loyalty providers our merchants use, end to end: the service that talks to the provider, the store plugin that applies the benefit to the cart, and the checkout UI that shows the balance, applies it automatically and works for guest shoppers too.
- **Payments.** Shipped a customer-credit payment method, covering sign-in, credit check, installments and tokenized payment, and worked on Apple Pay, gift cards and installment flows.
- **Shipping and addresses.** Pickup flows, alternative shipping estimates, and the confirmation flow for when a shopper's postal code doesn't match their cart.
- **Platform and tooling.** CDN caching for merchant settings, filters in the internal merchant console (Go + React), back-office monitors and k6 synthetic checks.
- **Less code to maintain.** Removed 15k+ lines of legacy integrations, an outdated benefits experience and one-off merchant workarounds.

Around 180 merged pull requests since mid-2025. Most of it lives in private repositories; the [checkout launcher Chrome extension](https://github.com/gabrielMatosBoubee/trinio-script-extension) is public.

### 🧩 Open source and deco.cx

- **40 merged PRs to [deco.cx](https://github.com/deco-cx)** ([apps](https://github.com/deco-cx/apps/pulls?q=is%3Apr+is%3Amerged+author%3AgabrielMatosBoubee), [deco](https://github.com/deco-cx/deco/pulls?q=is%3Apr+is%3Amerged+author%3AgabrielMatosBoubee)): commerce integrations for VTEX, VNDA, Magento and Algolia; SEO (canonical URLs, meta robots, sitemaps); the Konfidency reviews app; redirects in the framework.
- **Storefronts on deco.cx** for fashion and retail brands, such as [FARM Rio](https://github.com/deco-sites/farmrio/pulls?q=is%3Apr+author%3AgabrielMatosBoubee).
